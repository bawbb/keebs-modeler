# Expression

## Touch

- Key velocity changes both loudness and timbre: soft notes are dark and round, hard notes are bright and forceful.
- The change from softest to hardest is continuous — no audible steps or layers.
- Repeated notes on a still-sounding key respond to the new strike while the string is still ringing.

## Release and sustain

- Releasing a key ends the note naturally, not with a cut.
- The sustain pedal lets notes ring and lets the instrument resonate as a whole.
- Partial pedal is meaningful, not just on/off.
- Partial pedal tames loud notes while letting a quieter tail sustain.
- How the pedal's travel maps to damping is adjustable to suit the pedal and the player.

## Feel

- Latency is low enough that it is never felt while playing.
- Fast passages, trills, and dense chords stay clean and playable.

## Open

- Other expressive inputs (aftertouch, mod wheel, expression pedal, MPE) may drive any character control.
