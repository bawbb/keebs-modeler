# Vision

A piano-like instrument, played from a MIDI keyboard, whose sound is generated from a model of struck strings rather than recordings.

## Priorities

1. **Expressiveness** — the player's touch is audible in every note.
2. **Responsiveness** — it feels immediate under the fingers.
3. **Dynamic range** — soft and hard playing sound like different worlds, with every shade between.
4. **Character diversity** — controls that let a player shape a voice that is their own.

## Non-goals

- Faithful reproduction of any real piano.
- Being bound by a real piano's physical constraints. Where the real instrument is fixed, this one may be adjustable.
